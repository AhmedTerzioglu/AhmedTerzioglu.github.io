KVKK Information Notice

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) · November 14, 2026

1. Data controller

Ferkan Terzioğlu (developer of Wildhollow). Contact: gallerytycoon@gmail.com

2. Personal data processed

  • Identity/account: player ID (UID); e-mail and name shared by Apple or Google if you sign in with them.

3. Purposes

Creating and managing your player account, storing your progress and restoring it across devices, information security, fixing errors, answering requests, and showing optional rewarded ads.

4. Legal grounds

KVKK Art. 5/2-c (performance of the contract – providing the game), 5/2-ç (legal obligation), 5/2-f (legitimate interest – security and bug fixing), and your explicit consent under Art. 5/1 and Art. 9 for the advertising ID and transfer abroad.

5. Method of collection

Automatically and electronically through the mobile app when you play, and through the Apple/Google sign-in providers.

6. Transfers and transfer abroad

Data is shared with Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase hosting and authentication) and, if you choose that sign-in method, with Apple Inc. and Google LLC. Their servers are located abroad (EU and USA), so your data is transferred abroad. This transfer relies on your explicit consent given on the first launch of the game (KVKK Art. 9). Without consent, cloud save and account features cannot be used; you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

7. Retention

As long as your account exists; deleted immediately when you delete your account and removed from backups in the normal cycle.

8. Your rights under KVKK Art. 11

To learn whether your data is processed and request information, learn the purpose, know third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred, request correction, deletion or destruction, request notification of these to third parties, object to results against you arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage due to unlawful processing. Send requests to gallerytycoon@gmail.com; they are answered free of charge within 30 days.

Privacy Policy

Wildhollow · Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Wildhollow (the "Game") handles your information. The Game is developed and published by Ferkan Terzioğlu ("we", "us"). Contact: gallerytycoon@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

  • Account identifier. When you start the Game, an anonymous player ID is created through Google Firebase Authentication. No name or e-mail is needed to play as a guest.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google (optional). If you choose to link or sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique account identifier and, if the provider shares it, your e-mail address (Apple may give a private relay address) and display name. We never receive your password.
  • Game progress. Your hero, level, items, gold, statistics and settings are stored on your device and, for signed-in players, as a cloud save in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so you can restore it on another device.
  • Public hero profile. To populate the world and the Bloodfield arena, a public profile of your hero is stored in Cloud Firestore and shown in other players' games as a computer-controlled character: hero name, class, level, arena points, combat statistics (health, damage, speed and similar) and equipped items. It contains no e-mail address, real name or device data (unless you chose your real name as hero name). It is updated about once an hour while you play and is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Technical data. Our service provider (Google Firebase) may process technical data such as IP address, device type, operating system version and request logs to operate and protect the service.
  • Advertising (rewarded ads). The Game may offer optional rewarded ads. If ads are enabled, an advertising provider (Google AdMob) may process your device's advertising identifier and similar data. On iOS this happens only if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise only non-personalized ads are shown.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or health data. The Game has no chat and no in-app purchases at this time.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your player account and keep your progress (performance of the service you request).
  • To restore your progress on another device or after reinstalling.
  • To keep the Game secure and fix errors (legitimate interest).
  • To show optional rewarded ads (your consent where required).

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for automated decisions with legal effects.

3. Service providers and international transfer

Data is hosted by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore – database location: Europe, eur3). Google may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States, under its standard contractual safeguards. Apple and Google process sign-in data under their own privacy policies.

4. Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. Anonymous guest accounts that are never used again may be removed. When you delete your account, your cloud save and account record are deleted immediately from our systems; backups held by our provider are erased in their normal cycle.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the Game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, cloud save and the progress stored on the device. You can also e-mail gallerytycoon@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". See Account deletion.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 "KVKK"), you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Write to gallerytycoon@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority. Players in Türkiye: see the KVKK Information Notice.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device. Each player can only read or change their own cloud save. Public hero profiles can be read by other players of the Game but changed only by their owner.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the Game.

Delete your account

Wildhollow · November 15, 2026

Delete inside the game

  1. Open Wildhollow and tap Settings (gear icon).
  2. Go to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you may be asked to confirm with Apple once more.

What is deleted

Your player account, your cloud save, your public hero profile and the progress stored on the device are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone. Nothing is kept except copies in our provider's backups, which expire in their normal cycle.

Can't open the game?

E-mail gallerytycoon@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" from the e-mail linked to your Apple or Google sign-in, or include your Player ID shown in Settings → Account. We delete it within 30 days.
